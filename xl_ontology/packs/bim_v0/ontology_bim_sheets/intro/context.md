# Intro / background sheet

An intro sheet describes the model at a high level — what is being evaluated, in which population, in which country, over what time horizon.  It is mostly text; tables (if any) are small key/value pairs naming the intervention, comparators, indication, perspective, model type.

## Detection signals

- Sheet name matches one of the lexicon terms above.
- High text density, low formula density.
- Cells in rows 1–20 contain prose paragraphs (cell length   > 40 chars common).
- May carry a logo or company branding — not load-bearing.
- Often the first sheet (left-most tab).

## What lives here

- Intervention name (drug being assessed).
- Comparator names (drugs being displaced).
- Indication / disease area.
- Country / payer perspective.
- Time horizon (3 / 5 years typical).
- Brief modelling-approach notes.
- Sometimes assumptions list and references.
