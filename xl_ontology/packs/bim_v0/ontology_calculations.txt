ontology_calculations.txt
=========================

1. IDENTITY
-----------
ontology_pack: bim_v0
ontology_node: calculations
version: 0.2
model_family: BIM
expected_scale: 1-3 main calculation tables per workbook (MVP);
                future versions will recognise per-component patterns
llm_budget_share: 1 planner call + up to 3 defect calls = 4 calls max
                  per workbook (seed phase)


2. PURPOSE
----------
Identify the engine of the BIM — tables where inputs are combined into
per-year, per-treatment, or per-component values that feed result tables.
Calculation tables are the "middle" of the DAG: high fan-in from inputs,
high fan-out to results.

This agent does NOT enumerate every formula-bearing cell. It identifies
the table-level engines where the model's main arithmetic happens.

For framing, see ontology_calculations.context.md and
pack_overview.context.md.


3. EXPECTED CONCEPTS
--------------------
- total_cost_with_intervention      sum of all costs in intervention
                                    scenario, by year
- total_cost_without_intervention   same for comparator scenario
- cost_component_calc               per-component breakdown (drug, admin,
                                    AE, hospitalisation, monitoring) for
                                    each scenario
- patient_flow                      eligible × market share × duration =
                                    treated patient count per year
- scenario_calc                     calculations driven by scenario
                                    switches
- aggregator                        any table summing or weighting other
                                    calc tables to produce totals


4. STRUCTURAL FINGERPRINTS
--------------------------
- Upstream `node_type` is `calc` or `mixed`
- High formula density (≥ 50% of body cells contain formulas)
- High DAG fan-in (multiple input tables feed it)
- Non-zero DAG fan-out (feeds something downstream)
- Sits between input-tagged and output-tagged tables in the DAG
- Title or sheet name matches lexicon
- Sheet names: "Engine", "Calculation", "Calc", "Costs", "Cost per
  Patient", "Total Costs", "Per Patient Cost", "Annual Cost", "Workings"
- Time-indexed columns (Year 1 … Year 5) or cycle-indexed columns

Specific fingerprints:

- patient_flow:       formulas multiplying population × share × duration
- cost_component:     formulas referencing one input from a drug-cost-
                      style table, multiplied by patient count or dose
- aggregator:         SUM or SUMPRODUCT across other calc tables; small
                      bbox; near results


5. LEXICON
----------
Match case-insensitively.

total_cost:          total cost, total spend, total budget, total
                     expenditure, overall cost, gross cost
intervention_cost:   intervention cost, new drug cost, with intervention,
                     scenario A cost, treatment arm
comparator_cost:     comparator cost, current practice cost, without
                     intervention, scenario B cost, baseline cost
component:           drug cost, admin cost, monitoring cost, AE cost,
                     hospitalisation cost (in calc context — formula-
                     bearing, not input-only)
patient_flow:        treated patients, patients on treatment, patient
                     numbers, eligible × share, uptake-adjusted population
aggregator:          subtotal, total, summary, grand total, all-cost,
                     cumulative
per_patient:         per patient, per person, unit cost calc, average cost
annual:              annual, per year, year 1, year 2, yearly


6. DECISION RULES
-----------------
A. STARTING SET
   Pull all tables with upstream `node_type=calc` or `mixed`.

B. ENGINE IDENTIFICATION
   The "main" calc table usually has:
     - Highest combined fan-in × fan-out score
     - Time-indexed structure (columns = years)
     - Both intervention and comparator columns or rows present
   Identify top 3 by this score.

C. AGGREGATOR DETECTION
   Aggregators are short tables (1-5 rows) just upstream of result
   tables, dominated by SUM-style formulas. Identify any table with:
     - Formula density > 80%
     - DAG fan-in > 3
     - Bbox area < 50 cells
     - Direct edge to an output-tagged node
   Always include these.

D. CROSS-CHECK BY EXPECTED CONCEPT
   For each expected concept, confirm at least one candidate covers it.
   If not, search input set and output set with scope=whole_table —
   sometimes patient_flow lives inside an input-tagged or output-tagged
   table. The whole_table scope covers title + headers + row_names +
   body cell values + formula text. When a hit is found in a
   non-calc-tagged table, set `node_type_override_proposed: true` and
   call record_node_type_override.

E. STRUCTURAL SANITY CHECK (deterministic, no LLM)
   For every table in the workbook, evaluate:
     formula_density > 0.50 AND dag_fanin > 0 AND dag_fanout > 0
   Tables matching this pattern sit in the middle of the dependency
   graph and are formula-heavy — the calculation engine signature.
   When the predicate fires AND upstream label is "input" or
   "data_table", treat the table as a calculation candidate, set
   `node_type_override_proposed: true`, and call
   record_node_type_override. This check runs once per candidate after
   rule D, before rule F.

F. STOP CONDITION
   Stop after 3 calc candidates OR LLM budget exhausted. Excess
   formula-heavy tables go to `unclassified` with high priority.

G. HEURISTICS
   Executor checks ontology_calculations.heuristics.md for applicable
   heuristics before marking concepts absent.

H. DEFECT FLAGS (v0)
   If a candidate table carries upstream defect flags (title_missing,
   header_missing, rowname_missing, multi_core_detected), the executor
   calls the corresponding stub procedure (spec Section 10) and emits
   a defect_procedure_skipped trace event. The agent proceeds with
   whatever metadata exists; if classification is impossible, the
   table goes to unclassified with reason
   "defect_procedure_disabled_in_v0". Real defect recovery is deferred
   to a future xl_table_repair pre-stage.

8. FORMAT CONVENTIONS
---------------------
- Formula cells:        number/currency format, contain formula, often
                        locked, often plain (no fill) — distinct from
                        input cells which are usually highlighted
- Header cells:         text, bold, may be merged, often time-indexed
- Row label cells:      text, left-aligned, sometimes indented for
                        hierarchy
- Subtotal rows:        formula cells, often bold, often top border
- Grand total rows:     formula cells, bold, often double border or
                        distinct fill

Every extracted cell tagged with `format_role` (formula | header |
label | subtotal | grand_total).


9. CONFIDENCE CALIBRATION
-------------------------
- high:     upstream `node_type=calc`, lexicon match strong, fan-in and
            fan-out > 0, no defect flags
- partial:  upstream `mixed`, OR weak lexicon match, OR fan-out is 0
- inferred: LLM-classified after defect procedure
- absent:   expected concept not found anywhere


10. UNKNOWN POLICY
------------------
- If no calc tables found at all, model may be unusual (lookup-only).
  Mark `present: false`. Do not invent.
- For multi-core tables, use Section 10.4 of the spec — send whole
  table plus all formulas to LLM.
- Orphan calc tables (high formula density, zero fan-out) are unusual.
  Flag — may be deprecated or scratch. Do not include in main candidates.
